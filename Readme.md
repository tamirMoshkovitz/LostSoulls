# Lost Souls

> _"In an old basement – time stands still,  
> Dust on objects, on memories grown ill.  
> A heavy silence, with whispers beneath,  
> A gentle presence that hides in the sheath.  
> If you listen closely, through fracture and fold –  
> You might uncover what’s broken… and old.  
> And if you insist, with patience and grace,  
> You just might mend what once lost its place."_

---

## 🕹️ About the Game

**Lost Souls** is a short first-person escape room experience set in the eerie basement of an old Japanese apartment. Guided by the subtle presence of a *kami* — a spiritual being from Shinto mythology — you must explore forgotten objects, solve environmental puzzles, and uncover what has been lost to time.

This project was created as part of the **Game Lab** course in the **HUJI × Bezalel Game Jam program**, focusing on mythology and wandering as core themes.

---

## 🎮 Controls & Gameplay

- **Playtime**: 5–15 minutes  
- **Input**: Gamepad recommended  
  - For full experience with **PlayStation controllers**, connect via **USB cable** to enable **rumble feedback**
- **Genre**: Atmospheric puzzle exploration  
- **Theme**: Japanese mythology – inspired by the belief in *kami* (spiritual presences)

## 🧭 Exploration & Myth

The game was developed around the concept of **wandering within the unknown**, drawing from the **Shinto belief in *kami*** — unseen spirits that inhabit natural and forgotten spaces. Rather than direct storytelling, *Lost Souls* invites you to listen, look closer, and interpret.
